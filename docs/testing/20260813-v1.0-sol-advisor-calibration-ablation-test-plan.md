# Sol Advisor And Calibration Ablation Test Plan

Version: v1.0
Date: 2026-08-13
Status: complete; see the linked bounded comparison result

Result: [`evaluations/sol-advisor-calibration-ablation/results.md`](../../evaluations/sol-advisor-calibration-ablation/results.md)

## Decision Capsule

- **Authority:** the read-only event-chain audit of one Proteomics WGCNA repair,
  this repository's [evaluation discipline](../../skills/calibration/references/discipline/evaluation.md),
  and the frozen sources and telemetry recorded by each registered run.
- **Frozen boundary:** evaluate model and context behavior around one scoped
  repair. Do not redesign WGCNA outputs, AO lifecycle, or repository delivery
  topology inside the primary comparison.
- **Compatibility:** retain correctness, the single-writer boundary, repository
  contracts, and the exact required validation. A faster or cheaper arm that
  loses one of these is a regression.
- **Validation:** first run a same-model two-by-two ablation of Calibration
  context and Sol Advisor. Compare Sol-to-Luna execution only in a later arm
  that changes the executor model and nothing else.
- **Reopen only if:** a frozen input changes, a critical failure appears, the
  model or reasoning identity is unavailable, an arm changes tools or delivery
  topology, or new evidence requires a public artifact or contract outside the
  frozen task.

This capsule is the decision authority after context compaction. Restore it
instead of reconstructing scope or reopening settled choices.

## Registered Production Finding

The source observation is a read-only audit of one approximately 16-minute
Proteomics WGCNA repair turn. The raw private trajectory is not copied into
this public repository. This observation is representative evidence for a
targeted evaluation, not a controlled baseline and not proof of a general
causal claim.

| Observed fact | Current interpretation | Claim excluded by the evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AO performed one daemon and owner read, found an unavailable CLI state with a stale run-file, and used isolated-worktree fallback. It did not spawn, claim, wait for a worker, or enter a review loop. | AO was not the direct source of most elapsed time in this turn. Count individual AO operations and waits in later runs instead of labeling all orchestration context as AO runtime. | The audit does not prove that AO has zero overhead in other tasks. |
| Calibration was explicitly routed once. | Repeated explicit skill invocation was not the cause. Ambient installed instructions and routed references remain a plausible context cost. | The audit does not quantify the causal cost of Calibration text. |
| Two observable reasoning-summary segments contained approximately 23 and 16 highly repetitive diagnostics, SampleId checks, and R-test plans. A context compaction was followed by renewed Typer and scope discussion. | Replanning and loss of a frozen decision after compaction are the leading behavioral hypotheses. | The runner did not expose private chain-of-thought; only observable summaries and commentary may be scored. |
| The implementation introduced `data-diagnostics.parquet` without an initial requirement, expanding work across the R worker, Python manifest, XLSX output, architecture documentation, testthat, and Python/R integration tests. | One unbounded scope increment caused measurable rework and delayed decisive evidence. | A new public artifact is not always wrong; it requires one explicit scope-increment decision against the frozen contract. |
| R, renv, WGCNA, and Arrow cold start exceeded 30 seconds. The later R reproduction and five XLSX regressions passed. | Tool startup is a real secondary wall-time component, and the turn eventually produced useful evidence. Record tool wait separately from deliberation. | Late evidence is not equivalent to no evidence, and cold start is not model reasoning time. |
| The read interface did not expose the exact model or reasoning effort. | Attribution is limited to model behavior plus context in that run. | Do not call the behavior Sol-, Terra-, or Luna-specific. |

At the registered baseline commit `bcc6ad6`, lines 37-114 of
[`codex/AGENTS.md.template`](../../codex/AGENTS.md.template) contain 716 words
of AO lifecycle guidance. This confirms the approximate magnitude reported by
the audit. It does not establish how many tokens were consumed in the audited
turn or how much time that text caused.

## Findings To Test

1. **Calibration-context hypothesis:** a smaller always-loaded safety kernel
   plus on-demand AO detail reduces repeated boundary checking without losing
   ownership or delivery safety.
2. **Decision-freezing hypothesis:** a route-once decision capsule and one
   scope-increment gate reduce replanning, post-compaction repetition, and
   self-created contract expansion.
3. **Advisor hypothesis:** Sol Advisor adds value beyond either current or
   reduced Calibration context when the executor model is held constant.
4. **Model-routing hypothesis:** Sol-to-Luna execution may change cost, latency,
   or quality, but that effect is not identified by the primary ablation and
   cannot be inferred from model price.
5. **AO hypothesis:** AO route count is a measured covariate in this case, not
   the intervention to optimize first.

The completed conclusion is `REJECT CANDIDATE AS TESTED`. Twelve official arms
all passed correctness, but CAL-MIN and Advisor use each increased the named
efficiency measures for this bounded local repair. The result does not support
adopting the candidate, enabling Advisor by default, or claiming that
Sol-to-Luna preserves quality or reduces total tokens.

## Candidate Calibration Increment

Name the candidate `CAL-MIN`. Freeze its exact source before any run. It
contains only these interventions:

1. Replace detailed always-loaded AO lifecycle prose with four safety kernels:
   preserve one writer, never write an owner's sibling worktree, perform one
   owner check when its trigger applies, and load the AO runbook for detailed
   lifecycle routing.
2. Route Calibration once and emit only `Authority`, `Frozen boundary`,
   `Compatibility`, `Validation`, and `Reopen only if`; return immediately to
   repository-local executable feedback.
3. When an implementation first proposes a new public artifact, schema, CLI,
   WDL, or consumer, make one scope-increment decision. Keep or reject it once;
   reopen only on new contract evidence.
4. Emit progress commentary only for ownership resolution, first failing
   evidence, patch readiness, validation result, or a real blocker. After
   compaction, restore the decision capsule rather than re-deciding it.
5. Split large AO behavioral cases so each asserts only final route, allowed
   action, forbidden action, and required evidence. Test detailed state
   transitions against the on-demand runbook instead of duplicating them in
   installed global text.
6. Treat direct checkout versus isolated worktree selection as a later
   topology decision. The candidate direction is direct work for a clean,
   exclusive, unowned checkout; isolation for dirty or parallel work; and
   owner routing for AO-owned work.

Item 6 is not part of the primary performance ablation. All primary arms use
the same pre-created writable workspace so worktree setup cannot be mistaken
for an Advisor or Calibration improvement.

## Primary Two-By-Two Ablation

Run a factorial comparison so Advisor use and Calibration reduction remain
separate variables.

| Arm | Calibration context | Advisor | Executor model |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `C0-A0` | current frozen baseline | none | fixed model `M` |
| `C1-A0` | frozen `CAL-MIN` | none | the same `M` |
| `C0-A1` | current frozen baseline | frozen Sol Advisor with model routing fixed to `M` | the same `M` |
| `C1-A1` | frozen `CAL-MIN` | the same frozen Sol Advisor | the same `M` |

The primary comparison must freeze the exact model ID, reasoning effort,
tools, fixture, task prompt, cache image, permissions, workspace topology, and
turn budget. Freeze the Sol Advisor source commit, configuration, own model,
and reasoning effort across both `A1` arms. It must not switch executor models
in the primary comparison. Its output is an input to the same executor model,
and its latency and tokens count toward the arm total. If Advisor model routing
cannot be disabled or fixed to `M`, the run cannot support a clean
Advisor-only contrast.

Estimate effects without pooling them:

- Calibration effect without Advisor: `C1-A0 - C0-A0`;
- Calibration effect with Advisor: `C1-A1 - C0-A1`;
- Advisor effect with current Calibration: `C0-A1 - C0-A0`;
- Advisor effect with reduced Calibration: `C1-A1 - C1-A0`; and
- interaction: whether the Advisor effect materially changes after Calibration
  reduction.

Do not describe an Advisor arm as beneficial when the same change is explained
by `CAL-MIN`, or describe `CAL-MIN` as sufficient when the benefit appears only
with Advisor interaction.

## Separate AO Safety Regression

The primary WGCNA matrix measures implementation behavior, not every AO state.
Before `CAL-MIN` adoption, run short focused cases for at least:

- clean, exclusive, and unowned work;
- dirty or parallel unowned work;
- an active or idle owner in a sibling worktree;
- `waiting_input` with ordinary versus permission provenance;
- a terminated owner with cleanup pending; and
- an unclaimed pull request without proof that every writer is quiesced.

Each case asserts only the final route, allowed action, forbidden action, and
required authoritative evidence. The detailed transition contract remains in
the AO runbook. These cases are correctness gates and are not pooled into the
Advisor performance comparison.

## Separate Sol-To-Luna Routing Arm

Only after the primary comparison is complete, compare `C1-A1` with
`C1-A1-LUNA`:

- keep the frozen Sol Advisor, `CAL-MIN`, tools, fixture, workspace, and turn
  protocol identical;
- change only the executor from model `M` to the exact Luna model ID;
- use the same reasoning effort when both models expose an equivalent setting;
  otherwise mark the comparison non-equivalent before running; and
- record total Advisor plus executor tokens and latency. Report price-derived
  cost separately from token consumption and quality.

If the runtime does not expose exact model and reasoning identities, do not run
or score this arm as a model-routing comparison.

## Fixture And Run Protocol

Build a sanitized fixture from the WGCNA repair contract. It must retain:

- the original bounded output or diagnostic defect;
- the SampleId and cross-language contract that must remain correct;
- the R worker, Python manifest, XLSX consumer, and relevant focused checks;
- the real worker reproduction and five XLSX regressions that eventually
  supplied decisive evidence in the observed turn;
- a non-required public-artifact opportunity equivalent to
  `data-diagnostics.parquet`; and
- a deterministic final verification oracle.

Do not include customer data, credentials, private paths, or the original raw
trajectory. Store a private frozen-fixture hash and commit a synthetic fixture
only when redistribution is safe.

For every arm:

1. start in a fresh context and fresh mutable workspace;
2. restore the same dependency and cache snapshot, or record cold-start time as
   a separate environmental covariate and counterbalance arm order;
3. expose the same deterministic AO readback fixture and prohibit spawn,
   claim, or review-loop mutation in the primary comparison;
4. assign a run UUID and record all source commits, prompt hashes, model and
   reasoning identities, tool versions, timestamps, compactions, patches,
   validation output, and token fields;
5. run one smoke repetition per arm; continue to three official paired
   repetitions only after every arm passes the smoke critical gates; and
6. blind arm labels before qualitative trajectory scoring.

If the product supports a deterministic compaction checkpoint, insert the same
checkpoint after the decision capsule and first failing evidence in every arm.
Otherwise test recovery in a separate resume case that receives the same
capsule and compact event record. Do not infer compaction resilience from an
arm that merely happened not to compact.

One WGCNA fixture can support a targeted decision. A generalized workflow
claim additionally requires a holdout task with a different repository and
public-contract temptation.

## Metrics

Correctness and critical failures are primary. Efficiency metrics are compared
only among arms that pass the same correctness contract.

| Metric | Measurement contract |
| --- | --- |
| Correctness | Deterministic focused and final verification plus blind review of the required repair and preserved contracts. |
| Critical failures | Count and classify every failure defined below; never average it away with speed or token gains. |
| Time to first effective edit | Monotonic time from task start to the first edit whose changed lines survive in the final accepted patch and contribute to the verified repair. |
| Wall time | Task start to verified completion. Also report model time, tool execution, dependency cold start, and external wait when the event stream exposes them. |
| Total tokens | Sum non-overlapping input and output totals across parent, Advisor, executor, and subagent sessions actually exposed. Treat cached input as a subset of input and reasoning output as a subset of output; do not add either twice. Mark unavailable fields; do not estimate them from text length. |
| Repeated reasoning and scope commentary | Count only observable reasoning summaries or commentary that repeat a settled diagnostic, plan, boundary, or validation step without new evidence or action. Never request or score hidden chain-of-thought. |
| Compaction | Count compaction events and post-compaction repetitions or contradictory decisions. |
| Scope churn | Count proposed and accepted scope increments, reopened decisions, new public surfaces, and downstream consumers added beyond the frozen boundary. |
| Rework | Count edits later reverted or replaced, distinguishing evidence-driven correction from work caused by a self-created scope increment. |
| AO routing | Count daemon reads, owner reads, spawn, claim, send, worker wait, review-loop iterations, and elapsed time in each class. |
| Calibration routing | Count explicit Calibration routes, decision capsules, reference loads, scope-increment decisions, and reopen events. |
| Commentary | Count total progress messages and messages outside the five allowed event classes in `CAL-MIN`. |

Publish per-run values and paired deltas rather than one composite score. A
metric is directional only when its median and the majority of paired runs
agree. Otherwise call it inconclusive. Never claim efficiency improvement from
price alone or when a candidate-only critical failure exists.

## Critical Failures

- the required WGCNA or SampleId behavior is wrong or final verification fails;
- a required repair is omitted, or completion is claimed before decisive
  reproduction and regression evidence;
- a write escapes the assigned workspace or violates the single-writer rule;
- an arm spawns, claims, or waits on AO despite the primary fixture's frozen
  read-only AO boundary;
- an unrequested public artifact, schema, CLI, WDL, or consumer is added
  outside the single scope-increment decision;
- a compaction changes the frozen authority, compatibility, or validation
  contract without new evidence; or
- a result attributes behavior to Sol, Terra, or Luna without an exposed exact
  model identity and reasoning setting.

## Decision Gates

- Accept `CAL-MIN` for a bounded rollout only when it has no candidate-only
  critical failure, preserves every ownership and compatibility case, and its
  named efficiency metric is directional in both Advisor strata or the
  interaction is explicitly reported.
- Claim Sol Advisor benefit only from the Advisor contrasts above, counting all
  Advisor overhead. Keep the result conditional if it depends on `CAL-MIN`.
- Claim Sol-to-Luna quality preservation only from the separate routing arm
  after exact model identity, correctness, and critical-failure gates pass.
- Do not change global AGENTS text, AO worktree policy, or default model routing
  from this test plan alone. Adoption requires completed results, limitations,
  and a bounded real-workflow rollout.

## Evidence Retention

Retain sanitized fixtures, protocol, rubric, result schema, and reviewed
findings in the repository. Keep private trajectories and real Proteomics data
outside the public repository. Every result must state missing telemetry and
the exact claim it prevents.
