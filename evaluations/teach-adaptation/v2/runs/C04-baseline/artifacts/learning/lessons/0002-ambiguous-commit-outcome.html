When the commit answer goes missing

Your win: decide whether a failed request is safe to retry blindly.

The failure

A Python service inserts a payment row and sends COMMIT. The client times out before receiving the database response. The service cannot tell whether PostgreSQL committed before the connection disappeared.

One distinction

Confirmed rollback means the attempted transaction did not take effect. Ambiguous outcome means the client lacks evidence either way. A timeout describes what the client heard; it does not prove what the database did.

Your turn

The service sees this timeout. What should its retry path do first?

Retrieval prompt

Without looking back, say one sentence beginning: “A timeout is not a rollback because …” Send that sentence to your agent for feedback or ask any follow-up question that remains unclear.
